Your videos and score photos go to private Cadenza storage. Only Eric opens them, on a password-protected review page.

We never post them on social media or YouTube, and we never share them with anyone else.

Everything is deleted automatically 90 days after upload. Email music@cadenza-london.com to have it deleted sooner.

Before you upload

  1. 1

    Camera from the side. Landscape, so we can see your hands, face and feet on the pedals.

  2. 2

    Quiet room, light in front. Don't stand the phone on the piano itself.

  3. 3

    Introduce yourself. At the start of piece 1, say your name, grade and exam board.

  4. 4

    One take per piece, unedited. Play each piece straight through, like the real exam.

Private by design

  • Only Eric watches them. Your videos are never published or shared.

  • Stored securely. Kept in private storage, not on social media or YouTube.

  • Deleted after 90 days. Automatically, or sooner if you ask.
