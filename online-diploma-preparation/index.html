Good to know

Diploma preparation: your questions

Can I prepare for a piano diploma online?

Yes. Eric prepares students online for Trinity ATCL, LTCL and FTCL, and ABRSM ARSM, DipABRSM and LRSM performance diplomas in piano and violin, with live 1-on-1 lessons on a video call.

Do you prepare students for Trinity digital diploma exams?

Yes. Eric helps you choose a programme that meets the current syllabus, learn every piece, and record your performance to submit to the exam board.

How much do diploma lessons cost?

Lesson fees depend on your level and lesson length. Book a free 20-minute consultation or email us and we will confirm your fee. Between lessons you can also send videos for written and video feedback through Online Coaching: £35 for one diploma piece, or £129 for a Full Exam Check.

Will you help me choose my diploma programme?

Yes. Choosing a programme that suits your current playing and meets the board's requirements is the first part of preparation.

My playing has slipped since my last exam. Is that a problem?

No. Eric often teaches adults returning to the piano and builds your playing back up gradually before the diploma repertoire.

I live outside the UK. Can I still have lessons?

Yes. Lessons are online worldwide and are scheduled on UK time, Monday to Saturday. Tell us your time zone when you book.

How long does diploma preparation take?

It depends on your starting level and how much you can practise. Eric gives you a realistic timeline after your assessment lesson.

Can lessons be in Cantonese?

Yes. Lessons can be taught in English or Cantonese.
